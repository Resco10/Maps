Mountain House 2

The sequel to the original Mountain House, with the base built directly into a mountain and featuring redstone systems, farms, storage and more.

Features

Redstone entrances2x2 entrances at the front and back of the base.
Lighting systemRedstone lighting throughout the base with dedicated switches.
Crafting RoomCrafting and utility tables, furnaces, smokers and shulker storage.
ArmoryArmor closet with armor sets, tools and weapons, plus a double bed.
Farm RoomAutomatic sugar cane and bamboo farms.
Smelting RoomTwo super smelters: blast furnace and standard furnace systems.
Storage RoomLarge storage area connected to the enchanting and portal room.
Enchanting & PortalEnchanting table, Nether portal and themed Warped/Crimson decoration.
Trading HallTrading area with all villager professions, 13 villagers and one extra slot.
Farm DistrictCrop farms, four animal pens and dedicated farm storage.
