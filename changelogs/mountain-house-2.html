Update V1.1 — The Farm District

Changes & Improvements

  • Updated to Minecraft Version 26.2
  • Doors: levers now work both ways correctly; side entrance lever repositioned.
  • Added pressure plates with a welcome message and updated information board.
  • Updated main entrance with a Smooth Quartz Stairs header.
  • Expanded the Nether showcase beside the portal.
  • Patched holes and caves around the mountain.
  • Moved the portal/enchanting room light switch above the enchanting table.

New Additions

  • New Farm District.
  • Crop farms: wheat, potato, carrot, beetroot, pumpkin and melon.
  • Four animal pens: cows, sheep, pigs and chickens.
  • New Farm Storage under the farms, with a corridor planned to connect further into the base.
