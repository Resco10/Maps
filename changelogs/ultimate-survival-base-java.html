Update V2 — Ultimate Survival Base

17 December 2025

Changes

  • Replaced the white building with a two-story storage room matching the main base.
  • Added a new rear entrance between the Armory and Crafting Room.
  • Repurposed the old storage room into a crop farm.
  • Redesigned the Crafting Room.
  • Expanded Armory storage from 7 to 14 double chests.
  • Replaced Leather Armor with Copper Armor and repositioned Chainmail Armor.
  • Added four paintings and more lantern lighting.
  • Rebuilt the fountain using Waxed Oxidized Copper.
  • Updated signs to newer versions.

Removed

  • Secret area in the Enchanting Table Room.
  • Hidden chest/book used to open the secret area.
  • Carpets in the Armory, Super Smelter and Crafting Room.

Notes

  • A nearby Woodland Mansion was damaged early in the map’s history and can still be explored.
  • The map originated on Bedrock; some converted Java chunks may look unusual.

Hotfix V1.1

11 May 2025

Changes

  • Updated to Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.5.
  • Removed signs from the white building; iron doors remain.
