Update V3

31 December 2025

New Features

  • Main room redecorated into the new Crafting Room.
  • All crafting and utility tables added.
  • Upstairs redesigned into a compact Armory with all armor sets.
  • Old Crafting Room replaced by a Villager Trading Hall.
  • Trading Hall connected directly to the Smelting Room.
  • Decorative plants added across the base.
  • Improved lighting throughout.
  • Item frames with icons added to storage chests.
  • Third-floor room updated to lead directly to Animal Pens.
  • Chicken pen added.
  • Welcome signs updated.
  • Water outside the base removed.

Fixes

  • Updated map to Minecraft 1.21.11.
