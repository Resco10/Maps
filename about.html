About Me

The Story So Far

It all started back in 2020 during the pandemic. That summer, I came across a Minecraft map that someone had created years earlier, and it gave me the idea to completely revamp the build in my own way. That project became Mountain House, the first map I ever uploaded.

I had made other builds before then, but Mountain House was the first one I actually released publicly. From there, I kept creating new maps for both Minecraft Java Edition and Bedrock Edition, often with the help of friends along the way.

Since then, the projects have continued to grow through new maps, updates, improvements and new ideas including Survive to Glory, Starter Survival, Ultimate Survival Base and Mountain House 2. This website brings them all together in one place.
